What CHIM does

CHIM is a large AI framework for Skyrim built around meaningful gameplay with NPCs rather than isolated chatbot conversations.

  • Talk to vanilla NPCs, modded NPCs, followers, and even stranger edge cases like creatures or horses.
  • Use voice or chat-box input while CHIM tracks live world context around the conversation.
  • Customize personalities, voices, and behavior per NPC or per profile.
  • Integrate your own mods into the framework with custom biographies, Oghma data, and roleplay rules.
  • Start and advance supported vanilla quests through natural dialogue instead of navigating quest menus.

All NPCs

AI control is not limited to one hand-picked companion. NPCs around the player can be brought into the system automatically, with biographies and profile data keeping their roleplay grounded.

Generic NPCs also benefit from naming support like Real Names Extended and Tamrielic Names, so CHIM can treat them as characters rather than disposable background actors.

Background Life

NPCs keep doing things when you are not talking to them. They can form inner thoughts, send letters, spread rumors, and move between major locations based on what is happening in the world. They can also work, rest, trade, spend time with other characters, and use what they carry; when they are close by, that behavior can play out in front of you instead of only appearing in the log.

Use the web or in-game Background Life dashboard to choose who is tracked, view the map and history, send letters, trigger new actions, and manage rumors without leaving your playthrough.

Relationships And Growth

NPCs can build and lose trust, change their view of people and groups, and evolve over time through memories, diaries, relationship tracking, and ongoing interaction. NPC diaries can also become readable books in the game when that profile option is enabled.

Diary options →

Total AI Control

Pick different service providers, tune settings deeply, and assign different rules or model behavior to different NPC groups or profiles, from grounded roleplay to faster combat-friendly setups to stranger experimental behavior. Most of it can be adjusted without leaving the game, through the in-game Settings hub.

Action permissions are configurable per action. You can allow an action to run automatically, require an in-game Prisma confirmation, or disable it entirely.

Director Mode And Actions

Through actions and Director Mode, NPCs can carry out requests, react to situations, and create unscripted moments: social scenes, follower behavior changes, movement requests, or broader player-directed chaos using nearby NPCs.

NPCs can also reason from richer game state, including modded equipment slots, inventory keywords, recently collected items, and objects held in a VR player's hands.

Act on the world yourself with the CHIM Interact power: aim at a person or object, add an item or magic if you want, and describe what you do.

How CHIM Interact works →

Oghma, Narrator, And Extra Systems

Oghma Infinium gives NPCs lore knowledge that fits their background instead of making everyone equally omniscient. The Narrator adds a separate AI presence that can recap, guide, and comment on your adventure.

CHIM also supports speech, voice generation, persistent Soulgaze scene descriptions, Prisma UI, server plugins, custom Skyrim mod integrations, and tiny-model helpers like Minime-T5.

Oghma Infinium settings →

Direct Scenes And Keep Your History

Guide a scene or keep separate AI histories for different games.

  • Director Scenes lets you describe a scene for nearby NPCs to perform.
  • Smart Wait lets you describe what happens while you wait, then the Narrator tells the scene.
  • Playthrough Saves keeps AI history organized by character.
  • Voice Tools lets you preview effects and adjust pronunciation.
