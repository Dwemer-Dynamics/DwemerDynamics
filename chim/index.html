CHIM

Meaningful AI Gameplay

CHIM turns Skyrim NPC interaction into a broader AI gameplay layer with dynamic dialogue, world awareness, lore-aware roleplay, narration, and in-game actions.

Overview

CHIM is a large AI framework for Skyrim built around meaningful gameplay with NPCs rather than just isolated chatbot conversations.

  • Talk to vanilla NPCs, modded NPCs, followers, and even stranger edge cases like creatures or horses.
  • Use voice or chat-box input while CHIM tracks live world context around the conversation.
  • Customize personalities, voices, and behavior per NPC or per profile.
  • Integrate your own mods into the framework with custom biographies, Oghma data, and roleplay rules.
  • Start and advance supported vanilla quests through natural dialogue instead of navigating quest menus.

All NPCs

CHIM is designed so AI control is not limited to one hand-picked companion. Around the player, NPCs can be brought into the system automatically, with biographies and profile data used to keep their roleplay grounded.

Generic NPCs also benefit from naming support like Real Names Extended and Tamrielic Names, which helps CHIM treat them more like actual characters instead of disposable background actors.

Background Life

CHIM's Background Life systems let NPCs keep doing things when you are not directly talking to them. They can form inner thoughts, send letters, spread rumors, and move between major locations based on what is happening in the world.

They can also work, rest, trade, spend time with other characters, and use what they carry, and when they are close by that behavior can play out in front of you instead of only appearing in the log.

The result is that characters feel less frozen in place and more like they have a life outside the player.

Use the web or in-game Background Life dashboard to choose who is tracked, view the map and history, send letters, trigger new actions, and manage rumors without leaving your playthrough. See the Background Life guide to get started.

Relationships And Growth

CHIM tracks more than dialogue lines. NPCs can build and lose trust, change their view of people and groups, and evolve over time through memories, roleplay systems, and ongoing interaction.

This includes relationship tracking, diaries, memories, and the broader systems that let characters develop their own arc during a playthrough. NPC diaries can also become readable books in the game when that profile option is enabled. See Diaries for the available options.

Total AI Control

CHIM gives you broad control over how AI is used in your game. You can pick different service providers, tune settings deeply, and assign different rules or model behavior to different NPC groups or profiles.

The framework is meant to support very different play styles, from grounded roleplay to faster combat-friendly setups to stranger experimental behavior.

Most of that can be adjusted without leaving the game, through the in-game Settings hub. See Skyrim Settings for what it covers.

Action permissions are configurable per action. You can allow an action to run automatically, require an in-game Prisma confirmation, or disable it entirely.

Local Language Models

Use the launcher's LLM Studio component to run a language model inside DwemerDistro, or connect CHIM to a model app on another computer. See the Local LLM Guide to connect and test one.

Director Mode And Actions

CHIM is not only about talking. Through actions and Director Mode, NPCs can carry out requests, react to situations, and create unscripted moments in the world.

That can mean things like social scenes, follower behavior changes, movement requests, or broader player-directed chaos using nearby NPCs.

NPCs can also reason from richer game state, including modded equipment slots, inventory keywords, recently collected items, and objects held in a VR player's hands.

You can act on the world yourself with the CHIM Interact power: aim at a person or object, add an item or magic if you want, and describe what you do. See CHIM Interact for how it works.

Oghma, Narrator, And Extra Systems

Oghma Infinium gives NPCs lore knowledge that fits their background instead of making everyone equally omniscient. The Narrator adds a separate AI presence that can recap, guide, and comment on your adventure.

CHIM also includes support for speech, voice generation, persistent Soulgaze scene descriptions, Prisma UI, server plugins, custom Skyrim mod integrations, and tiny-model helpers like Minime-T5.

Direct Scenes And Keep Your History

Guide a scene or keep separate AI histories for different games.

  • Director Scenes lets you describe a scene for nearby NPCs to perform.
  • Smart Wait lets you describe what happens while you wait, then the Narrator tells the scene.
  • Playthrough Saves keeps AI history organized by character.
  • Voice Tools lets you preview effects and adjust pronunciation.
