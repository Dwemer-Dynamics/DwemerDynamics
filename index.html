Skyrim Special Edition / Skyrim VR

CHIM

Talk to almost anyone in Skyrim, give characters a memory of your adventures, and let them take part in quests and unscripted scenes.

  • Nearly anyone in Skyrim can join a conversation, from vanilla and modded NPCs to horses.
  • NPCs remember conversations and events, build relationships, and use Background Life to travel, write letters, and spread rumors.
  • Use Director Mode to arrange actions and scenes, and Oghma Infinium to give NPCs world and lore knowledge.

Kenshi

STOBE

Bring conversations and character voices into Kenshi, with dialogue that follows what is happening to your squad and the world around it.

  • NPCs remember what happens, keep diaries, and build relationships throughout your playthrough.
  • NPCs react to changes in the world and to the player's actions.
  • Give every NPC in Kenshi a voiced dialogue.

Fallout: New Vegas / TTW

Dialectic

Talk naturally with characters across Fallout: New Vegas and Tale of Two Wastelands, with dialogue presented through Fallout's own world and interface.

  • Nearly anyone in Fallout can join a conversation, including humans, non-feral ghouls, super mutants, and robots.
  • NPCs remember past conversations and events, and their memories stay aligned when you load an older save.
  • Any character can follow you and carry out actions such as traveling, trading, fighting, and picking up items.
