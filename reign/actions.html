Action sentence system

Build a clear request or offer. Review its people, targets and terms before sending it.

Development guide · November 24, 2026 · Edition notes

Build and send an action sentence

#

Open a supported Reign conversation with an eligible adult and press Alt+A once it is ready. Individual Chat, Party Chat, Correspondence, social events, the tavern house, supported court audiences, Royal Council, and a selected War Council messenger can host the builder. Select the actual participant. In Correspondence, Ctrl+M opens the inbox from the campaign map.

  1. Choose I would like you… to ask the other person to act, or I wish to… to offer or declare an action of your own.
  2. Choose the action, then fill in the people, destination, quantity, or other terms. Use the builder's search for a named action or target.
  3. Review the sentence. Use Back to change a choice. Enter advances an entry or accepts a ready draft, and Escape closes the builder.
  4. Accept inserts the protected sentence in your message. Review any ordinary speech around it and then use the host's Send control. In Individual Chat, Party Chat, and Correspondence, Enter or Numpad Enter also sends a ready message.
  5. Read the answer and check the outcome. An offer may be refused or countered. A counteroffer changes the terms and needs fresh approval. Look for the actual transfer, arrival, report, or other effect.

The lists below group actions by purpose. They include menu choices, offers that arrive through events, and follow-up controls. A choice can depend on the opening direction, eligible people and assets, current duties, location, authority, and feature settings. The chosen conversation may have further restrictions. Each row explains the situation and the choices or result. Some names in search may vary with the direction of the sentence.

Use Review offer from… when an NPC has made an actionable offer. Inspect its exact terms before accepting or choosing Decline this offer. To counter, change the term in review and send the revised sentence. To stop a running order, use its cancellation route. The protected sentence is one attached action. Reopen it through Alt+A to edit the draft, or remove it as a unit. Plain copied text carries no attached terms. Once focus returns to chat, pressing Enter can send the message.

The builder shows categories with choices available in the current conversation and direction. If a category or action is missing, check who acts, whom you addressed, whether they are free to act, and whether the feature and target are available. Some offers appear only when an event raises them. Try the exact action name in the builder's search.

Bargains and timing

#

A gift or request can end after one complete term. To negotiate an exchange, choose and I will give… or and you will give… in review. Add another action… combines compatible actions, and Edit a term or its timing… changes a selected term. A marriage proposal limits the consideration to the supported marriage assets.

Where the term allows it, choose execution as soon as delivery and the bargain allow, after a number of days, on arrival at a settlement, on release of a prisoner, on ownership of a settlement, or on peace or war with a named realm. Installments repeat the displayed amount at the chosen interval. Read the total before agreeing. Payment can be made in advance or after verified fulfillment where the builder offers that choice.

Remote terms can take time to deliver. Authority, stock, funds, and world state are checked before execution. Rebellion declarations and public pregnancy disclosures have their own immediate player declaration flow. For a negotiated action, check both consent and fulfillment.

Eligible immediate terms agreed face to face can start in the same meeting. A letter follows its courier schedule. Future dates, conditions, and reservations still apply. A package can reserve a named captive or guest while an eligible transfer is pending. Check the recorded state of each term before offering the same person in another deal.

Gifts and exchanges

#

Open Give… or use a give option in bargain review. The person supplying the term must own or lawfully control the selected asset. These actions can be gifts or pieces of a larger exchange.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Give goldChoose the payer, recipient, and amount. The payer must be able to fund the transfer.
Give an itemChoose the owned item, exact variant or eligible equipment slot, and quantity where applicable.
Give troopsChoose the troop type and number from an eligible roster. Check the parties and their available troops.
Give prisonersSelect captives in the giver's lawful custody. Transfer to the recipient's separate party or, for a named prisoner, to an authorized dungeon when the recipient is present there. The agreed destination must still qualify at delivery.
Give landSelect an eligible town or castle the giver can lawfully transfer. The transaction changes its ownership.
Give a workshopSelect a workshop owned by the giver. The recipient must qualify to take it over.
Give a shipSelect an eligible owned ship in a campaign that supports ships. Check transfer restrictions and location.

Personal and family actions

#

Use an eligible conversation with the people concerned. An NPC's personal offer can surface through its own interaction, with a review step for the agreed terms.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Leave your spouse by letterUnder I would like you…, ask a married adult NPC to send their current spouse a private separation notice. If agreed, it ends that marriage and can badly damage relations with the spouse's clan. The letter is the notice produced by the action.
Expose their concealed pregnancyUse I wish to… as the only action in the draft when your character has the required verified knowledge. Choose the confirmed case and whether to name the known father.
Arrange a private visitIn Visit the Madam, press Alt+A and select I wish to arrange a private visit…. Choose available adults and their agreed prices, then review the exact cast and total before confirming the visit.
Share this personal experienceReview an NPC's current personal-story offer when that event presents it. Acceptance applies to that specific offer.
Take personal leaveWhen the NPC offers personal leave, review their proposed departure and time away before agreeing.
Summon a subordinate for a meetingAsk a qualifying clan leader or ruler to summon their subordinate. Select the person, place, reason, and stay. Review the quoted arrival and any army release.
Change an agreed meetingIn a conversation eligible to control the live meeting, select it and agree to the new place and duration. The arrival time is recalculated.
Cancel an agreed meetingChoose the current meeting and agree to cancel. Reign releases the movement commitments made for that meeting.
Join my party as a permanent companionAsk an eligible known resident. Agree the exact payment, if any, and check companion capacity. Soldiers need duty release first.
Release resident from dutyAsk the eligible commander or home-owning clan leader to release the named military resident before recruitment.
Join my party as a temporary guestAgree in person with an eligible foreign noble or known resident. Set the purpose, fixed or open-ended stay, and any payment or army exit terms shown.
Extend your stay in my partyAsk a live guest who is still in your main party. Agree the revised term and relevant payment.
Leave my partyEnd the specific live guest agreement. The noble begins the return to their home party or other eligible destination.
Accept the risks of fighting your own factionAsk a current guest under I would like you… when they face this risk. Fighting their own faction can replace them in their old role, banish them, and move them into your clan.

Marriage actions and wedding choices

#

Open the exact offer through View marriage offer or address the issuing clan leader through Alt+A. The dedicated wedding choices appear only with a relevant offer or ceremony. See Arranged marriage offers and cultural weddings for the delivery window, town visit, and settlement sequence.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Propose an arranged marriageChoose an eligible leader, select your clan's prospect and theirs, and review the exact consideration before sending.
Release a captiveIn marriage bargain review, select a captive held by the side providing that term. The person goes free when the marriage settles.
Ask the prospective spouse to call off the weddingDuring the exact active ceremony, ask an eligible prospective spouse directly. The decision belongs to that person.
Marriage allianceUse the marriage action with the exact eligible consenting couple. In personal conversation this binds the actual participants. Clan offers for relatives use the dedicated proposal route.

For an incoming offer choose Accept this marriage proposal, Refuse this marriage proposal, or Request a meeting with… when offered. Select a safe town for the meeting and confirm the response to the issuing leader. Once the agreement is ready to book, the applicable choice is Marry now or Hold a ceremony. The ceremony also offers Withdraw from my wedding… for a participating player who chooses to end it.

Arrests and direct conflict

#

Address the accused for the arrest actions. Choose I wish to… to prepare an arrest, release a person, or rescind the case. A prepared arrest can lead to explicit confirmation, surrender, a duel, or a fight. See Arrests, confirmation, and custody.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Duel meAsk the current NPC to fight or spar. Choose Practice, Training Together, Honor, or Lethal. The prompt explains the mode before you enter the scene.
Prepare arrestUse I wish to… with the accused present. Enter the accusation, category, and severity. Confirm through the next arrest step.
Release arrested characterAs the player, select an applicable arrest case in custody. Release the person while leaving the accusation on record.
Rescind arrest accusationAs the player, select the case to withdraw. Custody ends if that case is what holds the person.
Attack your partyUse I wish to… after the accused refuses surrender in the prepared arrest. Select the case and eligible current party.
Attack my partyAsk a party leader to attack the player's party where the game permits the conflict.
Surrender to meAsk a free adult NPC in the same settlement or in your party to surrender personally when combat or siege restrictions allow. In an active encounter, the opposing party leader can surrender under the encounter's rules. Check whether the hero actually entered custody.
Leave me aloneAsk the actor to stop pursuing or threatening your party. This affects the current behavior.
Kill characterChoose the living character targeted by the execution and review who will carry it out. The game excludes the player character and can block execution through campaign protections.

Following and guiding

#

These requests use the available scene or campaign context. A guide leads the way in the world and waits for observed arrival.

ActionWhen and how to use it
FollowAsk an eligible actor to follow a person present in the scene or to follow the specified party on the campaign map.
Stop followingAsk the actor to end their current scene follow or campaign escort.
Show the waySelect a present person or recognized passage. Walk with the guide and allow them to register your arrival.

Party and military orders

#

Use Party and military orders… with the eligible commander, including via the War Council's messenger or a supported conversation. An order is supervised over campaign time. Check the chosen objective, targets, food, troops, army authority, and safe route. These orders command eligible NPC parties. Your main party can be an escort target, while you retain control of its movement. Identical objectives with alternate labels share a row. Attack settlement and Capture settlement plan have their own choices.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Issue campaign orderBuild a supervised order with a single objective or combine stages. Set each stage's target and requirements, then confirm with the commander.
Revise campaign orderChoose the active order and agree the new objective, destination, area, or timing. The same order keeps its history.
Cancel campaign orderChoose the active order and agree to stop it. Its previous accomplishments remain recorded.
Create partyAsk an eligible lord without a party to establish one. Creation still depends on authority, local troops and funding.
Go toChoose the town, castle, or village and have the commander travel there.
Hold positionEnter the campaign-map position to hold and confirm the coordinates before dispatch.
Wait near settlementPick the area and waiting time. The party waits under the order's safety rules.
Patrol around settlementChoose a center and patrol duration. Use for a bounded local patrol.
Scout reportSelect the settlement and time to scout. Read the report when the scout observes the area.
EscortChoose the party to accompany and the duration. This can include the player's party as the target where eligible.
Recruit and recoverSet a troop goal and food goal where offered. Choose a supply location or let the commander find an eligible one.
Form armySelect the gathering area and intended strength where offered. The commander needs authority, influence, and eligible contributors.
Join armyChoose an active army the commander is eligible to join.
Leave armyAsk a commander currently serving in an army to detach with the required consent and authority.
Disband armyAsk an authorized commander to disband the army they lead.
Raid villageSelect a hostile village and issue the raid order. Review the relationship and war consequences.
Besiege settlementChoose a hostile town or castle. The force must be able to reach and prosecute the siege.
Attack settlementChoose a hostile village to raid or a hostile fortification to besiege. The target type determines the order.
Capture settlement planChoose a hostile fortification and the relevant force goals. Use this for the capture plan and review its required stages and supplies.
DefendChoose a settlement and how long the commander should defend it.
Relieve siegeChoose an eligible besieged settlement and send a suitable force to relieve it.
Hunt enemy partiesSet a bounded period to hunt eligible enemies in the applicable campaign area.
Attack partySelect the exact enemy party. The commander must have a party and authority to engage it.
WithdrawOrder the party to disengage or move toward an eligible withdrawal point.
Return homeSend the party back to its eligible home or a named destination where the order permits it.

Clan and kingdom actions

#

Start with the party who can perform the action. Clan changes, service and accords require the relevant authority, an eligible counterpart and the terms the game accepts.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Hire me as a mercenaryAsk an authorized ruler to hire your clan. Select the kingdom and review the terms of service.
Release me from mercenary serviceAsk the responsible ruler to end your clan's current mercenary service.
Accept my oath of vassalageAsk the eligible ruler to accept your clan as a vassal. Review the kingdom and any agreed terms.
Release me from vassalageAsk the responsible ruler to release your clan from its current oath.
Join clanChoose the eligible receiving clan whose leader is the counterpart. This moves the person selected to act into that clan.
Leave clanAsk or offer to leave the actor's current clan when the native rules permit the change.
Join kingdomChoose the joining clan and eligible ruler's kingdom. The action changes clan allegiance.
Leave kingdomChoose the actor's clan when it can leave its kingdom under the current rules.
Hire mercenary clanArrange another clan's service with its leader and the hiring ruler. Select the clan and the hiring kingdom, then check the service terms.
Create clan accordAs a qualifying ruler, ask a willing noble to establish an available type of accord with their clan.
Cancel clan accordSelect the exact active accord and confirm ending it. Its benefits end. See Clan Accords for cancellation and relationship effects.

Rebellions and internal politics

#

Use the actual claimant, clan leader, ruler, or member involved. A pledge, an active rebellion and a completed political settlement are separate states. See Rebellion and civil war.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Declare rebellion against your ruleAs clan leader, address your ruler and use I wish to… in a draft of its own. Select the realm and your claimant clan. Seek any pledges before declaring.
Support me when I declare rebellionSeek an eligible clan leader's pledge before declaring. Name the challenged ruler. Their allegiance changes only if that rebellion begins.
Recruit lord to rebellionAsk the addressed lord to commit their clan to your active rebellion.
Join rebellionUse the player's side with the eligible NPC rebel leader to join their active movement.
Surrender rebellionAn eligible participant surrenders the named active conflict. Review the exact movement and the resulting state.
Resolve civil warUse the rebel and parent rulers to agree a negotiated end to their active civil war with recognition of rebel independence. The surrender action handles capitulation.
Consent government reductionSeek consent for the ruler's next reduction. A leader can speak for their clan. A clanless notable speaks for themself. See Government hearings and decisions for the separate reduction step.
Install ruling clanSelect the qualifying claimant clan after an internal challenge with the authority to install it.
Support claimantRecord support for the named claimant clan or pretender in the relevant kingdom.
Encourage clan defectionName the clan and destination realm when the eligible parties can agree to its transfer.
Exile clanSelect the affected clan under the realm authority that can expel it.
Restore exiled clanSelect an eligible exiled or independent clan and the realm restoring it.
Mediate clan disputeIdentify the clans in the dispute. An agreed mediation becomes a recorded political obligation or outcome.

Realm diplomacy

#

Open Diplomacy… with the ruler who can act for the realm. Most bargains also require the other participating ruler. The authority rules for a declaration, treaty breach, promise, or support for an active rebellion depend on that particular action. Choose the actual realms and check the completed treaty, transfer, or decision. Some actions record a commitment to fulfill later.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Declare warSelect the foreign realm and give a reason. The acting ruler must have authority to declare war for their own realm.
Back rebellionUse the sponsoring ruler and participating rebel leader in an active civil war. Name the movement and parent realm. The sponsor enters war with the parent.
Make peaceUse eligible opposing rulers to end their existing war on ordinary peace terms.
Offer tribute peaceSet the daily tribute and duration for peace. Check which realm pays before sending.
Record promiseThe acting ruler records a promise, the other realm, and the reason for future memory and obligations.
Demand reparations peaceSet the gold or tribute the other realm would pay for peace.
Demand settlement peaceSelect the enemy towns or castles to be surrendered for peace.
Demand surrender peaceReview the capitulation package with holdings, reparations or tribute. A defeated realm can lose its remaining land.
Sign trade agreementSet the trading parties and duration. Check the native agreement and Reign's treaty record after acceptance.
Sign non-aggression pactAgree a duration of restraint from attacks, raids, and hostile support.
Sign allianceUse the participating rulers and set the alliance term. Check that the game records the alliance.
Sign defensive pactSet a term of defensive support between the named realms.
Break treatyUse the acting ruler, name the other kingdom, and review the treaty breach and affected native ties.
Exchange prisonersChoose opposing realms with eligible noble captives. Both sides release captives under the agreed terms.
Ransom packageAgree the gold and the named captive nobles. Reign checks actual releases before paying and reports the custody changes it could verify.
Hostage guaranteeRecord an eligible diplomatic security obligation. Check the exact terms before treating anyone as transferred.
War indemnitySet the gold one kingdom pays the other for the war obligation.
Recognize conquestHave the eligible realm formally recognize another's conquest or claim.
Return occupied settlementSelect the occupied town or castle the acting realm currently holds and can return.
Demilitarized borderSet the term for border restraint against raids or sieges.
Caravan protection agreementSet a period for the two kingdoms to protect each other's caravans.
Supply agreementName the eligible resource or support, amount, and recipient realm. Check the recorded obligation and later delivery.
Loan or subsidyAgree the amount, payer, and receiving realm. Check whether the terms record a debt or a subsidy.
Pay to stay neutralSpecify the payment and period for the recipient realm to stay out of the dispute.
Pay to join warName the third realm and compensation. The receiving ruler must be able to enter that war.
Guarantee independenceSet the term of an eligible pledge to defend another realm's independence.
Protectorate or vassalageRecord the participating kingdoms' protectorate or dependency terms.
Diplomatic packageCombine compatible sovereign terms such as gold, captives, settlements, and peace. Review the exact package and available assets.

Government support and petitions

#

Use Support… with an eligible filer, member, sponsor, or other person who can supply the particular commitment. The controls resolve a real government, current party, petition revision, proposal or election. Supporting one step gives the commitment for that step.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Join political partyChoose an eligible party for the actor's political membership.
Back a party platformChoose the party and period of support for its platform.
Support a proposalChoose the active government matter and the exact vote option the member should support.
Support a party nominationChoose the nomination petition and party route. The builder binds the current revision and party selection.
Endorse a petitionAsk the eligible person to endorse the current petition. The exact revision matters.
Sponsor a petitionChoose the current petition draft and seek the eligible sponsor's support for that revision.
Seek party adoption of a petitionChoose the petition and obtain adoption through the applicable party route before presentation.
Present an adopted petitionUse the eligible presenter to submit the adopted petition to the appropriate government.
Consent to transfer a civic mandateChoose the eligible mandate petition and secure the exact participant's consent.
Nominate a party speakerSelect an eligible party candidate in the current speaker election.
Vote for a party speakerChoose the eligible candidate in the current speaker ballot.

A petition selector can offer Prepare a new petition…. Choose the realm and proposal, then use Save and submit for sponsorship. Follow the petition's later endorsement, adoption, or presentation steps where required.

Government hearings and decisions

#

Open Government decisions… with the person authorized for the exact hearing, decision, or contribution. A recommendation and a vote have separate effects. See Government and political participation.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Recommend an outcomeThe ruler selects a hearing and recommends a specific option. Members still cast their ballots.
Introduce a government proposalChoose the government and an available policy, fief review, or clan-expulsion proposal. It enters the hearing process.
Supply a government resolutionChoose the active resolution and quantity of eligible goods from the acting character's travelling party inventory.
Sponsor a petitionSelect the pending government business entry. This is the docket sponsorship route. The separate Support menu uses the petition draft and its current revision.
Postpone a hearingChoose an eligible hearing with its permitted recess available.
Reconvene a hearingChoose the postponed hearing when it can reopen.
Call a government voteChoose the hearing when the responsible official can call its actual member vote.
Decide a government hearingChoose the recorded option after the vote and applicable reconsideration period. Explicitly review an override if permitted.
Grant more governing authoritySelect the eligible kingdom and approve an increase in its government's authority.
Reduce governing authoritySelect the eligible kingdom and the reduction route. A forced reduction is a separate explicit choice where allowed.
Fulfill a government resolutionSelect an active resolution and an available route. Any agreed reward waits for recorded fulfillment.
Decline a government resolutionChoose the pending resolution and decline under the current decision rules.

Court offices and ambassadors

#

Use Court and intelligence… with the consenting NPC and the relevant royal authority. Appointments depend on the office, the person's availability, current duties and court access.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Serve as a court officerSelect Steward, Economic Advisor, Foreign Advisor, Marshal or Spymaster for an eligible person.
Leave your court officeAsk the current officer to end their appointment. An active Spymaster can have outstanding work that blocks departure.
Serve as ambassadorChoose another active kingdom and either public information or improving relations. The posting is fulfilled on arrival.
Return from your ambassador postingAsk the current envoy to return from an active posting.
Change your ambassador missionChoose public information or improving relations for the envoy's current posting.
Serve as ChancellorAgree the active-day salary with an eligible NPC. A new appointment begins inactive.
Relinquish the ChancellorshipAsk your current Chancellor to end the existing appointment through its court procedure.
Change your Chancellor dutiesAsk your current Chancellor to take active or inactive duties from the next day, subject to salary and court conditions. Emergency and handoff coverage have their own rules.
Serve as regentLegacy appointment entry. It uses royal court eligibility. For current court duties use the Chancellor route.
Relinquish the regencyLegacy departure entry that requires an active assignment. Current courts use Chancellor duties.

Spymaster missions

#

Use Court and intelligence… with your appointed Spymaster while the service has capacity. Choose the exact target and maximum operation cost. The ruler's available funds must cover the operation. Dispatch, detection, and success are recorded separately. Payment promised for success waits for the recorded result. A rumor is a reported claim. A reputation is an established label. Select the record you want to address.

ActionWhen and how to use it
Gather settlement intelligenceChoose an eligible settlement for an intelligence report.
Conduct a counterintelligence sweepChoose the settlement to check for covert interference.
Investigate a person's skillsChoose the person whose abilities you want assessed.
Investigate a person's relationshipsChoose the person whose social ties you want investigated.
Investigate rumors about a personChoose the person and await the report on their rumors.
Disrupt a settlement's food supplySelect the eligible settlement to target with food disruption.
Disrupt constructionSelect the settlement whose construction you want to impede.
Disrupt securitySelect the settlement whose security you want to undermine.
Disrupt loyaltySelect the settlement whose loyalty you want to weaken.
Attempt to assassinate a governorSelect the settlement with the eligible governor. Review the cost and risks of a lethal operation.
Attempt to assassinate a personSelect an eligible living adult target. Review the cost and risks before dispatch.
Mitigate a rumor about meSelect the specific rumor about your character to counter.
Mitigate my reputationChoose the specific reputation on your character's record to mitigate.
Mitigate a rumor about a personChoose the person and the exact rumor to counter.
Mitigate a person's reputationChoose the person and the exact reputation to mitigate.
Promote a positive reputationChoose a person and enter the exact favorable statement to circulate.
Circulate a harmful rumorChoose a person and enter the exact damaging claim to spread.
Circulate a severe reputationChoose a person and enter the exact severe allegation to circulate.

Follow-up controls and older action entries

#

The action system keeps separate entries for follow-up controls and older adapters. Follow-ups appear in their case or report flow. For the older transfer entries, the current conversation route is Give….

ActionWhen and how to use it
Confirm arrestUse the explicit follow-up after preparing a case. This step checks current custody and surrender eligibility.
Respond to order reportUse an urgent commander report to continue, adapt, hold, withdraw, or cancel the named order.
Resolve rebellion pledgeThe secret preparation flow records a pledge, refusal, or report when the relevant decision occurs.
Resolve rebellion summonsThe delivered summons flow records the player's response and the ruler's verdict.
Older trade packageUse Give… and add the other side's terms for a compound barter in conversation.
Older gold transfer entriesUse Give gold with the correct payer, recipient, and amount.
Older item transfer entryUse Give an item with the exact item and quantity.
Older workshop transfer entryUse Give a workshop for an eligible owned workshop.
Older prisoner transfer entryUse Give prisoners for custody transfer, or the relevant release, exchange, or ransom action.

The earlier Temporary Truce and Trade Embargo entries are retired. Use a supported non-aggression or trade agreement where it fits. Scene following uses Follow. Developer console and test commands have their own catalog and authority.
