What is still being verified

#

The guide covers the reviewed code and documented behavior, not a claim that every feature has passed release acceptance. Significant open areas include Royal Council, expanded Government participation and hearings, broader court-life work, NPC Lives journeys and missions, residents/Homes recruitment, personal gossip/communications, Clan Accords, Tavern House, ruler judgments, arena integration, and recent military/economic balance.

This November 24 edition incorporates changes in the paired Reign and ReignServer development sources. Your installed release may show older controls. The guide calls out the earlier clan caravan pilot where that difference matters.

Descriptions of these areas explain what their implemented routes are for, while Preview notices flag their limits. A roadmap checkbox, a successful build, a picture, and a natural-sounding reply are each different from final native acceptance. Your installed edition can also lag behind current development source.

Hardware planning figures remain provisional until representative campaign measurements are available.

The local installation includes the revised NPC matchmaking, Nord households, medical discharge, and war economy rules. The wedding evidence covers one complete Aserai route. The controlled war economy observations cover specific routes, with broader campaign balance still being tested. Preview notices indicate an edition difference or an open acceptance check.
