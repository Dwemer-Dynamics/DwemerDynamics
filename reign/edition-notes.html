What is still being verified

#

Your installed version may have different controls or feature availability. Preview notices explain where a feature still needs broader gameplay testing or behaves differently between versions.

Areas with open testing include Royal Council, Government participation and hearings, court life, NPC Lives journeys and missions, Homes recruitment, personal gossip and communications, Clan Accords, Tavern House, ruler judgments, arena integration, and military and economic balance.

Marriage and economy coverage includes specific tested routes; it does not establish every wedding route or long-term campaign balance. Read the NPC Clan Trade feature note for its availability in your version.

Hardware guidance remains provisional until representative campaigns have been measured. See Requirements before planning your setup.

Pictures marked In-game screenshot are player captures. Layout examples are illustrative references and may differ from the installed interface. Diagrams explain a process. Use the written steps and the controls in your game when an image differs.
