Arenas, tournaments, and participation XP

Enter practice fights and tournaments, and understand participation experience.

Browse A–Z · Search shortcut: Ctrl/⌘ K

Arena Overhaul is included

Preview included#

Use Reign's single launcher module. Arena Overhaul retains its own Arena Overhaul MCM settings section and established saved preferences. Do not assume that all arena controls live under the Reign AI settings.

The arena expands practice, loadout choices, companion participation, rewards, and tournaments. Talk to the arena master for the available practice routes. Fees, equipment options, and mode availability depend on those settings.

Practice modes and companions

Preview included#

Ordinary practice supports configurable participants, equipment, rewards, and experience. Expansive Practice broadens the exercise. Parry Practice focuses on defensive practice. Team Practice adds coordinated teams. Supported modes can let you play as a companion hero, and some can give viewing heroes experience when enabled.

Choose loadouts and weapon preferences where offered. Preferred or more expensive loadouts can carry a fee; random equipment is different from buying that choice. Defensive equipment and team settings alter the practice setup rather than permanently donating those items to your party.

These arena modes are separate from the keep's Training Yard and from a negotiated private practice duel. Use the one that matches whether you want to train troops, control a hero, practice a block, or spar with a particular NPC.

Tournaments and team victories

Preview included#

Tournaments can use broader prizes, prize quality and scaling, betting options, round rewards, and champion renown or influence settings. Team Tournaments have their own team-size and scoring controls. Reign's party-member team handling aims to let eligible accompanying heroes fight with you while keeping them out of opposing teams when you compete.

A team win matters as a team result in history, reputation, and celebration dialogue. An NPC winner's monetary award is separate from player prize variety. Current economy work changes NPC award accounting without removing the player's normal prize choices.

Regular tournaments also check your actual party roster while you compete, so a current temporary guest does not enter against you as an ordinary independent applicant. That protection does not continue for a former guest, or when you only watch.

The expanded elite prize pool includes ordinary high-quality weapons, armor, and mounts alongside eligible special rewards. Turning prize scaling off still leaves the expanded pool available. Recent prizes are avoided when enough alternatives exist, including after saving and loading. A prize already announced stays in place until the usual rules allow a new roll.

Preview: bundled arena integration, party-team eligibility, and the newest NPC reward revisions retain native acceptance follow-ups. Check the installed edition's available modes and do not treat an old design reference as a roster guarantee.

Participation XP

Preview included#

Meaningful completed spoken conversations can grant modest player Charm experience. Participating in a social-event phase can also provide a small learning bonus based on the actual people you spoke with. Deciding a real petition can grant Leadership experience.

These are player participation rewards, not unlimited experience for opening windows, replaying a reply, or making the same person appear more times in a phase. Normal Bannerlord learning modifiers still apply.

Control Center Options has an XP toggle plus overall and category adjustments. Conversation, event conversation, event learning, and petition rewards can be adjusted independently. Disabling a category does not bank old awards to reclaim when you turn it on again.
